Bedding

NATURAL FLAKE

  • Produced from a variety of coniferous softwood, predominantly spruce
  • Over 95% of particles fall within a 2-40mm size range
  • Wood fibre content of 99%+
  • Durable, long lasting and free from pathogens, pests and weed seeds

SMART HORSE

Smart Horse dust free shavings offer traditional bedding of the highest quality. Manufactured from kiln dried softwood, which is highly absorbent. Smart Horse wood shavings are free from all chemicals and make it safe for cleaning with allergies or respiratory problems.

BEDMAX

Large flake pine shavings, purpose made to provide an ideal bedding for horses and ponies.

100% natural bedding with the lowest possible levels of the airborne dust that can damage your horse’s respiratory system, and to help you create a bed that maximises hoof health, support, protection, hygiene and biosecurity.

SNOWFLAKE SOFTCHIP

  • Dust extracted for a healthy respiratory system.
  • Free draining and highly absorbent.
  • Quick and easy to maintain.
  • A naturally warm, soft and comfortable product with a pine fresh odour.
  • Environmentally friendly natural wood fibre. Sourced from sustainable resources in the UK, the bedding makes excellent compost after use.

SILVA BED

Made from 100% kiln dried pine, selected for its bright colour, high absorbency, low dust and exceptional ability to control ammonia in the stable.

TISSUE BEDDING

  • Perfect for puppies!
  • Ideal bedding for small rodents to rabbits.
  • Soft & warm
  • Recycled & dust-extracted
  • Biodegradable
  • Odourless
  • Very absorbent

SMALL BALES

For smaller pets, and when you don't need a large bale.

Easy storage, convenient to use, and at an affordable price.

PURE FLAKE

  • Traditional small flakes, white wood shavings
  • Kiln dried
  • Dust Extracted
  • Highly Absorbent
  • Great spread volume
  • Soft, cosy and comfortable for your horse

WOOD & SLUMBER

  • Small flake shavings
  • Dust Extracted
  • Kiln dried for absorbency
  • Soft & comfortable
